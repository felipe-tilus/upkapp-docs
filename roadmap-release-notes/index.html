    Roadmap & Release Notes

    Welcome to the Upkapp product log. Here you can track our journey, see what we have recently delivered, and explore the upcoming features planned to bring even more simplicity and power to your business routines.

    🚀 Release Notes

    Version 1.1.0 (Current Release)

    Focus: Collaboration, Templates, and Multi-Language Support.

    • Upkapp Templates: Added the ability to import and export pre-configured structures of Groups, Items, and Jobs. Users can now share custom blueprints using a unique Template Code or choose from our System Gallery.

    • Smart Merging: Template imports now safely merge into your existing configuration without overwriting or deleting any of your current data.

    • Job Restart Capability: Introduced the "Save and Restart" feature, allowing users to safely reset a Job's cycle to a new start date while keeping 100% of their historical compliance data intact.

    • User Localization: Every user profile can now independently select their preferred interface language (English or Portuguese).

    • Minor Bugs & Performance Improvements: Addressed several minor bugs and performance improvements.

    Version 1.0.0 (Official Launch)

    Focus: Core Engine and Intelligent Scheduling Framework.

    • The Four Pillars Structure: Launched our hierarchical data organization engine: Groups > Items > Jobs > Work Orders.

    • Smart Status Sorting: Implemented our proprietary prioritization algorithm that ranks tasks based on the remaining time versus your custom Advance Notice Window.

    • Dynamic Scheduling: Created dynamic recurrence logic that calculates the next task's due date from the actual completion timestamp instead of fixed calendar dates.

    • Counter & Data Tracking: Enabled usage-based task triggers (mileage, operating hours, cycles) featuring our manual 2-click, 5-second quick update dashboard.

    🗺️ Product Roadmap

    Our vision is to continue removing the mental load of repetitive processes. Here is a look at what our team is building next:

    prioritized features: On going

    • Developer REST API: Released lightweight API endpoints for automated meter/counter updates via external systems including custom software, IoT sensors or GPS/telematics trackers. Other endpoints will be released togheter.

    • Developer Webhooks: Released webhooks for automated notifications for when a Work Order is generated, updated or completed.

    Features to be implemented: waiting list

    • QR code to complete order: Added the ability to complete a Work Order by scanning a QR code generated by Upkapp.

    • attach pictures on work orders: Added the ability to attach pictures to a Work Order.

    • WhatsApp Automated notifications: Send notifications directly to your Staff users' WhatsApp numbers or group with work orders status.

    • improve reports and dashboards: Improve reports to show more information about the work orders and jobs.

    • Advanced Data Exports: One-click history extraction to polished CSV and Excel formats for accounting and auditing purposes.

    • Granular access control: Granular access control over items
