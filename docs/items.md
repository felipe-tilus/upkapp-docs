# Items

**Items** live directly inside Groups and represent the specific targets of your repetitive tasks and tracking. In Upkapp, an Item is a highly flexible concept: it can be a physical asset that requires maintenance, or a logical division (such as a legal or financial topic) that requires regular oversight.

By defining your Items clearly, you map out exactly *what* needs attention within your business operations.

---

## Physical Assets vs. Logical Divisions

Unlike rigid industrial software that only tracks physical machinery, Upkapp allows you to track anything that demands routine control.

### Physical Items (Assets)
These are tangible pieces of equipment, vehicles, or property that require hands-on upkeep to function correctly and safely.
* **Examples:** `Delivery Van #1`, `Main Server Rack`, `Office HVAC Unit`, `Forklift B`.

### Logical Items (Topics/Divisions)
These are intangible areas of responsibility, compliance requirements, or business processes that must be reviewed periodically to avoid legal or financial penalties.
* **Examples:** `Software Licenses`, `Corporate Passports`, `Monthly Accounting`, `Trademark Renewals`.

## How to Manage Items

### Creating a New Item
1. Navigate to the **Items** section in the main menu.
2. Click the **Add Item** button.
3. **Name:** Enter a clear name for the Item (e.g., `Main Office Elevator`).
4. **Group Assignment:** Select the appropriate parent Group from the dropdown menu (e.g., `Facilities`).
5. **Description (Optional):** Add extra context, such as serial numbers, model types, or specific notes that will help your team.
6. Click **Save**

Tip: Upkapp has info icons all over the app to quickly explain what each field means.

### Editing or Moving an Item
If your company structure changes, you can easily modify your Items:
* Open the Item from your dashboard and click **Edit**.
* You can update its name, description, or even change its parent Group if you need to reclassify it.
