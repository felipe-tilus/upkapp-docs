    Smart Status & Task Prioritization

    In typical task managers and calendars, items are sorted strictly by their scheduled due date. While this works for simple, linear to-do lists, it fails in business operations. A routine, low-impact task due tomorrow can easily push a critical, high-impact deadline due in a week completely out of sight.

    Upkapp solves this real-world operational issue with Smart Status, an intelligent prioritization algorithm that evaluates the contextual importance of each task, ensuring your most critical actions always rise to the top of your dashboard.

    How the Smart Status Algorithm Works

    Instead of looking only at the calendar deadline, Upkapp's algorithm continuously calculates an urgency score by weighing two distinct factors:

    1. The Due Date: The final date by which the task must be completed.
    2. The Advance Notice Window: A user-defined period that dictates how early you need to start preparing for, or be aware of, a specific task.

    By analyzing the ratio between the remaining time and your custom advance notice window, Upkapp determines which tasks represent the highest operational risk and dynamically rearranges your dashboard list.

    The Passport vs. Trash Example

    To see the power of Smart Status in action, consider how the algorithm evaluates two very different tasks on a manager's dashboard at the same moment:

    • Task A (Routine): Weekly Office Waste Disposal
      • Due in: 2 days.
      • Advance Notice Window: 1 day.
      • Context: It is a quick, low-impact task that requires no preparation.
    • Task B (Critical): Renew International Corporate Passport
      • Due in: 10 days.
      • Advance Notice Window: 30 days (because passport renewal involves bureaucracy and weeks of processing time).
      • Context: Missing this deadline halts critical international business travel.

    The Sorting Comparison:

    Dashboard View Top Priority (Rank #1) Second Priority (Rank #2)
    Standard Calendar Apps Task A: Waste Disposal (Due in 2 days) Task B: Passport Renewal (Due in 10 days)
    Upkapp Smart Status Task B: Passport Renewal (Deep inside its critical preparation window) Task A: Waste Disposal (Normal routine cycle)

    Why this makes a difference: Standard apps hide the passport renewal because it is 10 days away, causing managers to forget about it until it is too late. Upkapp recognizes that because the passport requires 30 days of advance notice, having only 10 days left means you are already running out of preparation time. It safely forces the passport to the top of your list.

    Understanding Work Order Statuses

    As work orders move through their lifecycle and the Smart Status algorithm evaluates them, they are assigned specific operational statuses. Upkapp dynamically sorts your dashboard based on these statuses, displaying them from the highest priority to lowest priority:

    1. Late: The scheduled due date has passed, and no action has been taken yet. This demands immediate attention to avoid operational, financial, or legal consequences.
    2. Not started: The wor order is now due but did not reach deadline to complete
    3. Started: The team has acknowledged the task and active work has officially begun within the scheduled timeframe and did not reacj deadline to complete.
    4. Started late: Work is started, not completed and have reached deadline.
    5. Upcoming: The task is approaching and has just crossed into your user-defined advance notice window, signaling that it is time to start planning or preparing for it.
    6. Scheduled: The task is safely planned for the future and currently sits well outside its advance notice window, requiring no immediate action.
    7. Cancelled: The specific work order was intentionally aborted and will not be executed during this cycle.
    8. Skipped: This specific work order was intentionally skipped. The system logs the skip for transparency and automatically focuses on the next scheduled cycle.
    9. Done: The work order has been successfully completed, verified, and closed out. The system immediately archives this instance for your compliance history and calculates the next cycle.

    How to Configure Advance Notice

    To optimize the Smart Status algorithm for your specific business risks, make sure to define the preparation window whenever you create or edit a Job:

    1. Navigate to Items and open an asset.
    2. Click Create Job (or edit an existing one).
    3. Locate the Advance Notice field.
    4. Input a realistic timeframe based on the real-world friction of the task:
      • Routine cleaning/checks: Set to 1 day or 0 days.
      • Vehicle oil changes/inspections: Set to 3 to 5 days.
      • Commercial leases or insurance renewals: Set to 15 to 30 days.
      • Complex legal certifications or passport renewals: Set to 30 to 60 days.
    5. Click Save.

    By aligning these notice windows with your actual operational realities, Upkapp turns your dashboard into a self-sorting command center that automatically protects your business from overlooked risks.
